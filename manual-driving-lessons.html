Learn to Drive a Manual, Your Way

Manual driving gives you full licence flexibility — the ability to drive any car, anywhere. Our DVSA-approved instructors teach clutch control, gear changes and road awareness through structured, one-to-one lessons in a modern dual-control car.

Every lesson builds on the last, so you always know what you're working towards. We track your progress against DVSA test standards, giving you honest feedback and a clear plan for reaching test-ready confidence.
